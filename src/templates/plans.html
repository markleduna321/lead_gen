KNOW BEFORE YOU SEARCH

How search credits work.

Search credits cover discovery work. Results vary by location, availability, filters, and businesses already saved; a particular number of leads, emails, or phone numbers is not guaranteed.

See charges, plan access, and refund details

Local discovery costs {{ local_price }} credits per industry, covering the selected sources included in your plan. Each selected industry adds its own charge. A submission is one accepted discovery run; daily caps apply to submissions across both discovery modes and do not replace credit charges.

Solo includes Google Maps and Yelp discovery. Small Team adds Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn company pages, and preset hiring searches. Enterprise also lets you create custom hiring services.

Small Team and Enterprise members share the owner's search credit balance and daily submission cap. Each account keeps its leads and outreach mailbox private. The owner occupies one seat; pending email invitations reserve seats until accepted, revoked, or expired.

Hiring discovery on Small Team and Enterprise costs {{ hiring_price }} credits per role-page: one role searched on both Google Jobs and LinkedIn Jobs for one page each, including company-contact lookups. The total charge scales with selected roles and page depth; an itemized quote appears before submission. Local and hiring discovery share the same credit balance.

AI pitch drafts on Solo, Small Team, and Enterprise cost {{ pitch_price }} credit{% if pitch_price != 1 %}s{% endif %} each, including regenerations, from the same credit balance. If the AI service fails, the credit is refunded automatically. The Free Trial covers discovery only.

For a completed search with zero new leads, {{ refund_percent | int }}% of the search charge is refunded as search credits, not cash; {{ retained_percent | int }}% covers processing. Refunds apply when the whole completed submission produces zero new leads, not to individual empty sources or industries within a submission that produces new leads.

Validation failures and searches that fail before returning results are not charged. Completed searches with zero new leads follow the 70% refund policy instead. The applicable charge and refund policy will be shown before submission.

i

Monthly and annual plans and credit top-ups are one-time purchases with no automatic renewal. Access is added after payment is confirmed. Contact support to change an active paid plan. New accounts receive a 7-day free trial with 30 search credits after email verification (one trial per network).
