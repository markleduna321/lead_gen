KNOW BEFORE YOU SEARCH

How search credits work.

Search credits pay for discovery work, not a guaranteed number of leads, emails, or phone numbers. They are separate from our provider's Scrapingdog API credits. Results depend on location, availability, filters, and businesses already saved.

Local discovery costs 10 credits per industry, covering the selected sources included in your plan. Three industries cost 30 credits, not 10. A submission is one accepted discovery run; daily caps apply to submissions across both discovery modes and do not replace credit charges.

Discovery access: Solo can select Google Maps and Yelp only. Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn company-page options remain visible but locked, with an Upgrade to Small Team prompt. Hiring Companies discovery also remains visible but locked for Solo, with the same upgrade prompt. Small Team can search the preset hiring services; Add Service remains visible but locked with an Upgrade to Enterprise prompt. Enterprise can create and search custom hiring services.

Small Team and Enterprise members share the owner's search credit balance and daily submission cap. Each account keeps its leads and outreach mailbox private. The owner occupies one seat; pending email invitations reserve seats until accepted, revoked, or expired.

Hiring discovery on Small Team and Enterprise costs 15 credits per role-page: one role searched on both Google Jobs and LinkedIn Jobs for one page each, including company-contact lookups. A four-role service costs 60 credits at one page per role, 180 at three pages, or 300 at five pages (Enterprise only). Local and hiring discovery share the same credit balance.

AI pitch drafts on Solo, Small Team, and Enterprise cost 1 credit each, including regenerations, from the same shared balance. If the AI service fails, the credit is refunded automatically. The Free Trial covers discovery only.

For a completed search with zero new leads, 70% of the search charge is refunded as search credits, not cash; 30% covers processing. For example, a 10-credit search receives 7 credits back (net cost: 3); a 15-credit search receives 10.5 back (net cost: 4.5). Refunds apply when the whole completed submission produces zero new leads, not to individual empty sources or industries within a submission that produces new leads.

Validation failures and wholly failed provider searches are not charged. Completed searches with zero new leads follow the 70% refund policy instead. The applicable charge and refund policy will be shown before submission.
